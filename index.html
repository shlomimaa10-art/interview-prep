Interview Prep
🎯

Your personal interview coach

Real pressure. AI that pushes back — just like a senior engineer would.

✨
AI-generated questions
No question? Hit Generate — the AI crafts a fresh system design question matched to your level and focus areas.
🧠
Real interview feel
The AI acts as a senior engineer — asks clarifying questions, challenges assumptions, and digs into trade-offs without giving anything away.
⚙️
Fully configurable
Set your level (junior → senior), pick a style (strict / balanced / friendly), and choose your focus areas. The interviewer adapts.
✏️
Live Excalidraw whiteboard
Draw system diagrams mid-interview using the built-in Excalidraw canvas. A tab on the right edge of the screen opens and closes the whiteboard drawer. Sketch shapes, arrows, and labels freely. Your diagram is automatically sent with every message — the interviewer reads it, asks about missing links, and probes gaps in your design.
💾
Crash-safe sessions
Your interview is auto-saved as you go. If your browser crashes or you accidentally close the tab, just reopen the app — chat, timer, and whiteboard pick up exactly where you left off. Every session you start also auto-lands in History so nothing is ever lost.
📚
History, Resume & export
Every past interview is saved automatically. Open 📚 History to view, ▶ Resume any prior session and continue exactly where it left off (chat, whiteboard, and timer all restored), or 📥 Export a Markdown copy with the full transcript and an embedded PNG of your whiteboard.
🏢
Company context
Add a short note about the company you're interviewing at on the Setup tab. The interviewer biases scale assumptions, scenarios, and trade-off questions toward what would actually matter at that company.
📊
Whiteboard-aware feedback
Type "feedback" for a structured evaluation that grades the diagram itself — calling out specific elements you drew well and gaps in the architecture — alongside the verbal answer. Calibrated 1-10 score, no defaulting to 7.5.
📚
Study Mode (Socratic tutor)
Open the 📚 Study tab to learn a topic the right way: a Socratic tutor that asks instead of lectures, with tiered hints (hint/I'm stuck/show me), a hint-level indicator, mastery bar, end-of-session quiz, and a topic-locked whiteboard for partial diagrams. After any interview, click the 📚 Study these next chips in your feedback to jump straight into a session on that gap.
How to use it
1
Get a question — paste your own or hit Generate to get one tailored to your level and focus areas.
2
Configure the session — set your level, style, focus areas, AI provider, and (optional) company context to bias the interviewer toward a specific company's domain.
3
Start the interview — the AI opens cold, no intro. Drive the design exactly like a real session.
4
Sketch on the whiteboard — click the ✏️ Whiteboard tab on the right edge of the screen to open the Excalidraw drawer. Draw shapes, arrows, and labels to sketch your architecture as you explain it. When the whiteboard has content it is automatically included with every message — the AI reads your diagram in real time.
5
Get feedback — type "feedback" at any point for a structured, honest evaluation of your answer.
6
Restart, export, or revisit — hit 🔄 Restart to redo the same question, 📥 Export to download a Markdown copy, or 📚 History to browse past sessions and ▶ Resume any one of them — chat, whiteboard, and timer pick up exactly where they left off.
Commands & shortcuts
"feedback"Stop and get a full structured evaluation — what you nailed, what you missed
"give full answer"Ask the AI to reveal the model solution
"hint"Get a nudge without giving the answer away

🔄 RestartReset the chat and timer for the same question — the previous run is saved into History
📥 ExportDownload the current session as a Markdown file (transcript + whiteboard PNG embedded)
📚 HistoryOpen the past-interviews modal — view, export, or delete any prior session
▶ ResumeIn the History modal — jump back into any past session and continue where it left off
